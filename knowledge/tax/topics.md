# Indian tax testing scope

This starter corpus is for prototype testing. Primary legislation must take
priority over tutorials and FAQs. Time-sensitive rates, forms, due dates,
notifications and circulars must be rechecked against the current official
portal before being shown as definitive.

## Direct tax topics

- Taxpayer status, residence and scope of total income
- Salary, house property, business/profession, capital gains and other sources
- Exemptions, deductions, rebates, set-off and carry-forward of losses
- Individual, HUF, firm, LLP, company, trust and non-resident taxation
- TDS, TCS, advance tax, self-assessment tax and refunds
- Return filing, revised/belated/updated returns and tax forms
- Assessment, reassessment, search, survey, recovery and refunds
- Interest, penalties, prosecution, appeals and dispute resolution
- Transfer pricing, international taxation, DTAA and foreign tax credit
- Charitable institutions, presumptive taxation and tax audit
- Virtual digital assets and reporting obligations
- Transition from the Income-tax Act, 1961 to the Income-tax Act, 2025

## Indirect tax topics

- GST levy, supply, time/place/value of supply and exemptions
- Registration, composition scheme and cancellation
- Input tax credit, blocked credit and input service distributor
- Tax invoice, debit/credit notes, accounts and records
- Returns, payment, interest, refunds and zero-rated supplies
- E-way bills, e-invoicing and job work
- Assessment, audit, inspection, search, seizure and arrest
- Demand, recovery, offences, penalties, appeals and advance rulings
- CGST, IGST, UTGST and compensation cess
- Imports, exports, customs valuation, classification and duty
- Customs assessment, warehousing, baggage, drawback and appeals

## Official secondary sources to add during scheduled refreshes

- Income Tax Department tutorials, FAQs, tax calendar, forms, circulars and notifications
- CBIC GST Acts, Rules, circulars, orders and rate notifications
- GST Portal help material and advisories
- India Code amendments and Gazette notifications
